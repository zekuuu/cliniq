**Functional Requirements** (grouped by module — see Section 5.3 for full module list):
- **FR-01** Role-based login for Staff (full read/write) and Admin/Principal (reads the Dashboard, reports, student profiles and incident reports; manages accounts; never prints; ADR-033, 038); PE/Sports Instructors have no account (ADR-029)
- **FR-02** Student record encoding with duplicate detection and mandatory-field enforcement; a yearly import of the registrar's list sets grade and section, and a student missing from the new list is archived after a review list is shown (ADR-047, 051); a visit or incident keeps the grade and section the student had when it was saved
- **FR-03** Teachers and school employees recorded as clinic patients with their own screens, identified by a Staff No. (ADR-043)
- **FR-04** Daily clinic visit logging with complaint, shown to users as "Symptoms" (one main symptom and up to 3 extra tags; labels only, ADR-070), medicine suggestions from item tags (ADR-071), treatment, medicines and supplies taken from inventory, and disposition tracking; a visit recorded by mistake is marked "Entered in error", never deleted; a visit can be escalated to an incident (ADR-036, 053)
- **FR-05** Excuse letter and PE/Sports referral handling; the clinician can approve a letter in one step and gets a warning, never a block, when it covers more than 3 days; medical certificates are explicitly out of scope (hospital-issued, not generated by CLINIQ; ADR-049)
- **FR-06** Two-stage emergency incident logging — essential fields captured fast, remaining detail completed afterward — plus hospital referral tracking and timestamped parent-notification outcome logging
- **FR-07** Follow-up handling for students told to return for monitoring, recorded inline after a visit or incident, with a Pending/Completed/Missed/Cancelled status and no dependency on external SMS or push notifications
- **FR-08** Automated monthly and incident report generation, with a weekly breakdown, a monthly inventory print, exact counts, and Sports Reports for PE instructors (ADR-034, 050); every printout is an A4 document printed by the clinician. The four monthly reports are Monthly Report, Incident register, Symptom summary and Inventory sheet; there is no Clinic Overview printout (ADR-074); the clinician sends the Principal the Monthly Report as a PDF (ADR-079)
- **FR-09** Student lookup by LRN or name from each form's student picker, so recording a visit or logging an emergency starts with the student already identified
- **FR-10** Screen-context display rule: multi-student list/dashboard views (visit logs, incident logs, frequent-visitor flags) show the masked LRN (last four digits), not the student's name, since these are the screens a bystander could glance at; a deliberate single-student lookup — full profile, search — shows the full name and the full LRN, and so do the printouts about one student (ADR-040, 044)
- **FR-11** Rule-based first-aid checklist prompts during visit logging
- **FR-12** Medicine/supply consumption logging from received batches, earliest-expiring batch first, with low-stock and nearing-expiry alerts and explicit disposal of expired stock (ADR-030)
- **FR-13** Clinic-wide operational dashboard with an Overview and a Trends view: visit/incident counts, pending records, health trends, a possible-cluster alert, frequent-visitor warnings and due/upcoming follow-ups; and a separate Activity calendar of visit and incident counts with school events (kept by Staff and the Admin/Principal) and national holidays (ADR-019, 020, 032, 042, 050)
- **FR-14** Daily automated backup to the internal and external drive locations the clinician chooses in the system, with a visible backup-status indicator, "Back up now", and a warning when the last good backup is over 48 hours old (ADR-037, 052)
- **FR-15** Audit logging of every login, submission, approval and print across all modules, a read-only Audit Log Viewer for Staff and the Admin/Principal, and a log entry when the clinician or Admin opens a student profile or incident report (ADR-016, 038, 048); audit summaries carry no names or LRNs and changes are recorded by field name (ADR-017, 058, 066)
- **FR-16** Password recovery with an authenticator app (6-digit code or one of 8 one-time recovery codes), set up by each user from the account menu; no email address on any account; the Admin reset with a temporary password stays the fallback (ADR-073)
- **FR-17** Sign-in rules: 5 wrong passwords in a row lock the account for 5 minutes; a new or reset account must change its password first; the session ends 7 days after sign-in however active the user is (ADR-015, 057)
- **FR-18** Idle sign-out per role, Staff after 60 and the Admin after 30 idle minutes by default, each a setting of 10 to 240 minutes edited by Staff on a Settings page, with a 2-minute warning and countdown (ADR-048, 078)
- **FR-19** Account menu in the header: every user changes their own display name, picture and password; username and role stay with the Admin (ADR-066)
- **FR-20** School years run June 15 to April 14 by default (a Staff setting); Staff end a school year and start the next by hand, with Dashboard reminders; one read-only Archive page shows archived students, staff, reports and audit entries by school year; archived records are kept 5 years and the audit log 10 years (ADR-051, 072, 075)
- **FR-21** Staff list import from a CSV that adds and updates only, and a staff record status with its own Incomplete Records page (ADR-067, 069, 076)
- **FR-22** The Admin's Reports page: a Report month drop-down and the four monthly reports, each opened as a view-only on-screen A4 sheet with masked LRNs; no print, save or export (ADR-077)
- **FR-23** Inventory items carry "Commonly used for" symptom tags that put matching medicines first on the Visit form; stock adjustments need a reason, the note is optional, and a large write-off is flagged in the audit log (ADR-061, 068, 071)
- **FR-24** Backup locations are added and disabled in the system, never edited or deleted; at least one location must be a drive kept away from the clinic room (ADR-052, 079)

**Intuitive shortcuts for frequent tasks** (added during vault setup, grounded in Nielsen's "flexibility and efficiency of use" heuristic — full detail: `04-Development/Keyboard-Shortcuts-and-Efficiency.md`) are additive to this list, not yet folded into the canonical Project Plan's bullet form.
