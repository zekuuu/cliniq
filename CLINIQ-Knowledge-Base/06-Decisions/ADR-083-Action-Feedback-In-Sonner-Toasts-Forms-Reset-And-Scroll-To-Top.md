## Context
Before this decision, each screen reported a result in its own way. The ways were: a `SaveFeedback` banner beside the button (6 screens), hand-made green and red panels at the top of a form (about 15 screens), one home-made corner notice in the shell (profile saved, issue submitted), and a message passed to the next screen through `lib/savedMessage.ts`. Several actions said nothing at all: sign out, the backup location toggle, archiving a record, copying the recovery codes, approving a letter or an incident report, and the calendar's event saves. Some failures were thrown and never shown. Forms were inconsistent after a save. New Visit, Add Student and Dispense cleared; the PE referral said it cleared but kept everything. Add Item and Add User kept the typed values, and none of them went back to the top. The app had no scroll reset on a route change, so a save that moved to another screen could land halfway down it.

## Decision
1. **Sonner** (`sonner` 2.0.8, MIT, React 19) is the one toast library. One `<Toaster>` (`components/ui/Toaster.tsx`) is mounted once in `App`, at the bottom right: `richColors`, a close button, three visible at a time, collapsed, light theme (the app has one), `containerAriaLabel="Notifications"`. It is offset 64 px from the bottom, above the "Scroll to top" button. On a phone it spans the width inside the 16 px gutters. Sonner's own z-index puts it above every `Modal` (z-40).
2. **One wrapper, `lib/notify.ts`, is the only module that imports `sonner`.** An ESLint `no-restricted-imports` rule enforces this, except in `notify.ts` and `Toaster.tsx`. It offers `success`, `error`, `warning`, `info`, `loading`, `promise`, `dismiss`, `checkFields` and `apiError`. Durations are set there: 4 s for success and info, 6 s for errors and warnings, 8 s for a toast with an action. Each toast has an id (by default its kind plus its message), so a repeated action shows one toast. A loading toast turns into its result under the same id.
3. **Errors go through one mapper**, `errorMessage` / `notify.apiError`. Status 0 reads "Cannot reach the server. Check the connection and try again." 401 reads "Your session ended. Please sign in again." 429 reads "Too many requests. Please wait a moment and try again." Any 5xx reads "Something went wrong on our side. Please try again." A 422 with field errors reads "Some fields need attention." For 403, 404 and 409 the server's own message is used, because `ApiExceptionRenderer` writes those for people; without one they read "You do not have permission to do that.", "That record no longer exists." and "Someone else changed this record. Reload and try again." The mock layer's own error messages are used as they are. Built-in JavaScript errors (`TypeError` and the like) never reach a toast; the screen's plain fallback does.
4. **Toast, inline or dialog:**
   - **Toast:** the result of anything the person did, success or failure: a save, a status change, a copy, a backup, a print that cannot start, sign out.
   - **Inline:** field validation stays beside the field, and focus moves to the first invalid one. One toast "Please check the highlighted fields" is added, sent from `useFocusFirstInvalid.report`, which every entry form already calls. A form-level check with no field of its own stays inline too, and its words become the toast's text. These stay inline as well: page-load failures with Retry, empty states, skeletons, and on-screen states such as "Voided", "Approved and stored", "No backup location is enabled" or a duplicate warning.
   - **Dialog:** confirmation dialogs stay dialogs; their result is a toast.
5. **Copy rules:** sentence case, one short sentence, past tense naming what was done ("Visit recorded", "Staff record archived"). No exclamation marks, no "successfully", no jargon, no emoji. An error says what failed and what to do next.
6. **Privacy:** CLINIQ holds the health records of minors, and a toast can be read over a shoulder. A toast never carries a diagnosis, symptom, vitals, medicine or other health detail. It names a record by its number or by the screen's own label, and a person only where the same screen already shows them. So after a visit, stock below zero is "Some stock is now below zero. Recount and restock in Inventory.", without the medicine's name. A stock refusal that names an item stays beside the items, with a plain toast. A dispensation for a patient reads "Dispensation recorded"; only general use names the item.
7. **Colour and time:** the toast colours are the ADR-062 meanings: the fill, the accent for the border, the text shade for the words. Sonner's icon and the words carry the meaning, never the colour alone. Each toast also has a 4 px line along its bottom in its accent colour. The line shrinks to nothing over the toast's time on screen (Zean's request), pauses while the pointer is over the toasts (as Sonner's timer does), and stays full under reduced motion.
8. **After success: reset, then the top.** A successful **create** form goes back to what a new form looks like: its own defaults (today's date, the last disposition), no picked student, no file, no errors. An **edit** form keeps the values just saved. A form that moves to another screen still moves, with its toast sent first. A dialog that closes on success still closes. A two-stage form (an incident) changes nothing until its final step, because each stage is a saved record. Then `lib/scrollToTop.ts` scrolls the window, or the open dialog's overlay, smoothly unless reduced motion is set, and moves focus to the main content. A failure clears nothing. Login keeps the username and clears only the password. Search, filters, sort and page size are never cleared. The app mounts React Router's `ScrollRestoration`, so a new screen starts at the top and Back returns to where a list was.
9. **The button keeps its own feedback.** `loading` ("Saving…") and the 2-second `done` check ("Saved ✓") remain on the shared `Button`; the toast is the message.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Keep the inline banners for errors as well as a toast.** Zean declined; two places for one message.
- **Remove the button's "Saved ✓" check.** Zean declined; it was his November 8 request and sits on the button, not beside it.
- **Clear an edit form to blank after a save.** Rejected: it looks as if the data was lost.
- **A form library (React Hook Form) for a generic "reset every input".** Rejected: the forms hold their state in plain `useState`, each already has its own defaults, and a library for this is a new dependency the request did not ask for. Each form resets its own state, and the shared parts are `notify` and `scrollToTop`.
- **Remounting the whole form by a `key`.** Rejected: it reloads the form's data and flashes the skeleton.
- **Sonner's `toast()` called from each screen.** Rejected: durations, ids, copy and the error mapping would drift.

## Consequences
- A new action sends its result with `notify`, never with `sonner` or an inline banner (the `cliniq-toast-patterns` skill). Field errors stay inline.
- Tests read a toast: `test/setup.ts` mounts the Toaster before every test, and `test/findToast.ts` finds one by its text.
- Two notices on Login stay inline: "You were signed out after N minutes of inactivity" and "Your session has ended". An idle sign-out happens while nobody is at the PC, so a toast would be gone before anyone read it.
- A toast can cover the bottom-right corner of a page for its few seconds, such as the pager of a long list scrolled to its end. It can be closed, swiped or hovered to pause.
- Canonical documents: the Frontend Design Reference and the Frontend Context Brief describe screen feedback. Both need a line saying an action's result is a bottom-right toast (this ADR); they are not edited here.
