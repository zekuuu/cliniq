## Format for new entries
When you find or resolve something, add it here with a date and enough context that someone with zero memory of the conversation that created it can still act on it.

**Adding:** something to fix or do gets `- [ ] **[TAG]**` with `FIX`, `AUDIT`, `DOC`, `DECIDE`, or `LATER`. Context only gets a plain `- **[NOTE]**` or `- **[VERIFY]**` bullet with no checkbox.

**Resolving:** change `- [ ]` to `- [x]`, change the tag to `DONE`, and add `Resolved <real timestamp>` plus the session-log link. Then **move the bullet, unchanged, to `Issues-Done-Archive.md`** under the same section heading (add the heading there if it is missing). Do not delete the bullet.

**Always:** update the "Open work at a glance" counts and the open `FIX` list in the same edit. **Also, for a new `DECIDE`:** add one line with its title to "Open decisions by who must answer", under the group of whoever has to answer; when it is resolved, remove that line there (the entry itself stays).
