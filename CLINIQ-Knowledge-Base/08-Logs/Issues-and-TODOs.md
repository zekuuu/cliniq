## Open design questions — school year handling & grade promotion

**This was a real gap: this whole discussion happened in chat and was never actually written down here — caught late, when it should have been captured the same session it came up, like everything else in this file.** Nothing below has been decided; these are the open questions as originally raised, not resolved answers.

**What's already in place, relevant to this:**
- **[NOTE]** Student Number stays fixed for life at the school (`YYYY-NNNNN`, tied to enrollment year, not current grade) — deliberately designed so promotion doesn't force a new ID.
- **[NOTE]** Archive, not delete, for students who leave — hidden from active lists, kept for up to 5 years post-archive, then deleted (data retention policy).
- **[NOTE]** Registrar's Office is documented as the source of student data, but only for the *initial* import — nothing describes an ongoing yearly process.

**What's actually undecided:**
1. [ ] **[DECIDE]** **Grade-level promotion at year rollover.** Does the system bulk-promote every active student one grade level at the start of a new school year? Who triggers it — Staff clicks a button, or does it happen automatically on a date? What about a student repeating a grade — how does Staff exclude them from the bulk promotion? Update Saturday, October 17, 2026 — 08:08 PHT: at the earlier meeting the Principal said students move up by academic performance and go automatically onto the new master list [I 10:08]. The school makes a new master list every school year, so promotion arrives with the yearly list, not through a clinic-side button (reading, not stated). How a student repeating a grade appears was not discussed. Session: [2026-10-17-principal-clinic-meeting-findings](Agent-Sessions/2026-10-17-principal-clinic-meeting-findings.md)
2. [ ] **[DECIDE]** **Section reassignment.** Sections often get reshuffled each year independent of grade level. Same question: bulk operation, manual per-student, or does this just come from a Registrar re-import? Update Saturday, October 17, 2026 — 08:08 PHT: sections are not fixed from year to year: in elementary, first enrolled and first paid decides the morning session or first section, parents may request another section, and reservations start around February [I 10:08]. Sections therefore change with the yearly list. Session: [2026-10-17-principal-clinic-meeting-findings](Agent-Sessions/2026-10-17-principal-clinic-meeting-findings.md)
3. [ ] **[DECIDE]** **A repeatable Registrar sync, not just a one-time import.** At the start of each year: some students are new (need a Student Number generated), some are continuing (need grade/section updated), some have left (need archiving). Is that one combined yearly process, or three separate manual actions? Update Saturday, October 17, 2026 — 08:08 PHT: the school makes a new master list every year, sometimes copied and pasted when the students are the same, but its arrangement changes because of transferees [I 03:50, 10:08]; the registrar tells the clinic about mid-year additions [I 07:55]. This is a real choice, not a default to assume either way.

## Format for new entries
When you find or resolve something, add it here with a date and enough context that someone with zero memory of the conversation that created it can still act on it.

**Adding:** something to fix or do gets `- [ ] **[TAG]**` with `FIX`, `AUDIT`, `DOC`, `DECIDE`, or `LATER`. Context only gets a plain `- **[NOTE]**` or `- **[VERIFY]**` bullet with no checkbox.

**Resolving:** change `- [ ]` to `- [x]`, change the tag to `DONE`, and add `Resolved <real timestamp>` plus the session-log link. Do not delete the bullet.

**Always:** update the "Open work at a glance" counts and the open `FIX` list in the same edit.
