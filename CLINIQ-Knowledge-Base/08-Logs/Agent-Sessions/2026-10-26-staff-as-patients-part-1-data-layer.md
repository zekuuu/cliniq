Changes Made:
- **Data design (shown to Zean before building, per the prompt):** `Personnel` = `id` (`personnel-0001`), `staffNumber` (`STF-0001`, system-assigned, never reused), `fullName`, `type` (a label from `config.personnelTypes`: Teacher, Non-teaching staff, Utility worker), `department` (null or at most 60 characters), `contactInfo`, `allergies`, `medicalConditions`, `emergencyContacts` (may be empty), `archived`. No LRN, grade, sex or completeness. Every field is PROVISIONAL, pending the ERD. The clinical records carry `PatientRef` (`studentId`, `personnelId`, both `string | null`). Seed rows may omit `personnelId`, and `resolveSeed` reads a missing one as null, so no existing JSON row changed.
- **Integrity:** each visit, incident and follow-up has exactly one patient; a transaction has one or none (a restock or a stock adjustment has none). A dangling `personnelId`, a malformed or duplicate Staff No., an unknown type, a department over 60 characters and a stored derived value (`recordComplete` and the rest) are rejected. A follow-up or a transaction must have the same patient as its visit or incident. An excuse-letter draft or an approved letter on a staff visit is rejected. The existing student rules are unchanged; the messages still contain "doesn't match its visit's student" and "must match visit", which the old tests look for.
- **Writes:** `recordVisit`, `saveIncidentStageOne` and `createFollowUp` take a student or a staff member, exactly one ("Choose one patient: a student or a staff member."). Escalating a visit to an incident requires the same patient. Medicine lines and `dispenseInventoryItem` write `personnelId` on the transaction. A staff visit cannot carry an excuse-letter draft, and `approveExcuseLetter` refuses a staff visit (ADR-043 item 7).
- **Staff functions (`api.ts`):** `listPersonnel` (search by name or Staff No., filter by type, archived hidden by default), `getPersonnel`, `searchPersonnel`, `searchPatients` (students and staff in one result list, each labelled `student` or `personnel`, capped at 8, nothing medical), `getPersonnelTypes`, `getPersonnelDepartments`, `getPersonnelHistory` (visits, incidents and follow-ups), `savePersonnel` and `archivePersonnel` (School Clinician only, `PersonnelPermissionError`). Writes are audited under the new target type `staff` ("Staff"): create, update with the changed field names, archive. The selector `patientSplit` gives the Students / Staff split for Part 4.
- **Audit Log selector:** a `staff` target, and a visit, incident or follow-up of a staff member, is labelled with the Staff No., never the name.
- **Keeping the screens unchanged:** `view()` in `api.ts` leaves out staff records (visits, incidents, follow-ups, stock transactions) unless a read asks for them (`includeStaff`). Every existing screen therefore reads exactly the student records it read before. Only `getPersonnelHistory` asks for them now. The guard is marked `ponytail:`, and Parts 3 and 4 remove it per screen. The Dashboard trends and the Frequent-visitor count skip staff records; that is also the intended end state (D14).
- **Seed (D17), all fictional:** 12 staff (5 teachers, 3 non-teaching, 4 utility; 3 with allergies, 3 with conditions, 2 with no emergency contact, `personnel-0012` archived); 20 visits (`visit-0160` to `visit-0179`, two today, at minutes no other visit uses); `incident-0024` at Stage 1 today and `incident-0025` complete with a reached emergency-contact call; follow-ups `followup-0015` (pending) and `followup-0016` (completed); dispense transactions `txn-0013` and `txn-0014`, which back the two staff visits with a medicine line.
