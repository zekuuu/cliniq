The audit ran on a one-year build with the old generator, on `cliniq_check_pest`, because `cliniq_load` is not granted. Its findings, all fixed in the generator's rules:
- **The build crashed on `main`.** Since ADR-084 (`StockService::take()` refuses more than the usable stock), the generator tried to give 2 face masks with 1 in stock. It now gives an item only when a whole dose is in stock, or it tries the next choice.
- **Ages against grades.** Birth dates were January 1 plus 0 to 364 days of one year, which ignores the August 31 cut-off, so **269 of 900** students fell outside their grade's band. `siblingsAndTwins()` also copied a birth date to a random student in another grade: the 9-year-old in Grade 8, a 17-year-old in Grade 8 and an 8-year-old in Grade 1. Now a Grade g student of the year starting in June of Y is born September 1 of Y−6−g to August 31 of Y−5−g. Twins share a family, a grade and a birthday. Siblings are at least two grades apart and share a surname, a middle name (the mother's maiden name), a guardian and a phone number. The three intended same-name pairs share a surname and are in different grades. Other names repeat only by accident of family, not of the draw: there were 64 accidental pairs before.
- **Roster.** It tapers (shares 78, 76, 76, 74, 72, 72, 70, 70, 68, 68, 66, 56, 54) and grows 2.5% a year. This year is the requested size plus 1%, so transfers keep it at 900+ (906 active at the end). Earlier years are smaller (about 810 in 2022). Each year some students leave at promotion (4% into Grade 7, 18% into Grade 11, 2% elsewhere), and new enrollees join mostly at Grades 1, 7 and 11.
- **Contacts.** The guardian is drawn by weight (Mother 55, Father 25, Grandmother 10, Aunt 4, Uncle 2, Guardian 4), with a first name that fits (all 165 "Father" contacts had female names before). Numbers use mixed real prefixes and are unique per family. Staff get real departments (not "Office" for every non-teacher) and mixed emergency relationships (all 65 were "Spouse").
- **Health records.** Conditions follow per-mille rates by grade: asthma 6%, allergic rhinitis 4%, eczema up to Grade 6, migraine from Grade 5; epilepsy and heart murmur are rare. Allergies include drug allergies. The 60 items now carry allergy words (paracetamol, ibuprofen/NSAID, mefenamic/NSAID, iodine/povidone), and the generator never gives a matching item.
- **Complaints and treatment.** Menstrual cramps go only to female students aged 10 and up and to female staff (62 impossible visits before). A complaint-to-item table replaces the random fallback, which had given an epinephrine trainer for eye irritation. Age gates mean syrups for Kinder to Grade 6 and tablets from Grade 4 or 7; adult strengths go to staff only. Doses fit the unit: 5 to 10 mL of syrup, 1 tablet.
- **Times and volume.** Half-hour slot weights peak after the flag ceremony, at recess and after lunch. Kinder visits only in the morning (half-day classes). There are weekday weights (Monday 19.4 a day, Friday 15.4), month weights, and season weights: rainy-month respiratory complaints 31–38% of visits, hot-month dizziness and fainting 13–17%. Calendar effects: Intramurals brings sprains and wounds, exams headaches, deworming day stomachaches. About 15 visits a school day per 900 students. Every visit has a time out: 10 to 25 minutes when returned to class, up to 2 hours when sent home.
- **Release, letters and follow-ups.** Dispositions are set per complaint (returned 66%, resting 18%, sent home 15.5%, referred 0.6%). A letter goes only to a sent-home or referred visit, by complaint. A follow-up only for fever sent home, a wound, a sprain or an allergic reaction, due on a school day (51 of 146 used to fall on weekends).
- **Vitals.** Visit temperatures use the settings' `feverFromC` and `temperatureMinC`: Fever reads 37.6 to 39.0, a wound reads normal (before, every complaint had a uniform 36.2 to 38.7). Incident pulse and blood pressure come from `vitals_usual_ranges` by age group, shifted by the event (fainting lowers the pressure, asthma lowers SpO₂ to 89–93), and Stage 2 moves halfway back. A Kinder no longer reads 119/83.
- **Incidents.** About 3.5 a month (it was about 4 a week), three times as likely to be injuries on Intramurals and Foundation Day. Events are filtered by grade. Asthma attacks and seizures mostly happen to the students who have the condition. Parent calls are 3 to 15 minutes apart until someone answers (1 to 3 calls). Referrals happen only for serious events, to generic destinations. One incident in twenty is completed the next school day, and the principal approves within one to three school days (before, 20% were never approved).
