Changes Made:
- **Cause, sidebar (the reported case).** The sidebar `<nav>` is `overflow-y-auto`; CSS then clips the horizontal axis too. The global ring (`outline: 2px`, `outline-offset: 2px`, unchanged) is drawn 4px outside a link that is exactly as wide as the nav, so the nav cut 4px off the left edge (right room was exactly 0), and 4px off the top/bottom of the first/last item when the nav scrolls. **Fix:** `-m-1 p-1` with `w-[calc(100%+0.5rem)]` and `pr-2` (was `pr-1`), plus `scroll-py-1`. All 11 Staff links keep the same position and width (x 16, width 235, same y at 1440×900). Measured room for "Audit Log": left −4 → 0, right 0 → 4. The collapsed rail (4.75rem) also gets `scrollbar-thin`: found only by the visible run on Zean's Windows screen, a full-width Windows scrollbar on the scrolling nav (short window) left 36px for the 40px icons, cutting their ring by 1.2px.
- **Phone navigation dialog:** the same pattern and fix (`-m-1 p-1 scroll-py-1`); room −4/−4 → 0/0.
- **Tables (`DataTable`):** the scroller is `overflow-x-auto`; an action flush with its edge lost 3–4px of ring. Fix: `-mx-1 px-1` (layout unchanged) and `scroll-px-1.5`; with pinned columns, the scroller sets `scroll-padding` to the pinned column's width on focus so a cell never scrolls under it.
- **Tables whose buttons overflowed their column:** Visits, Incidents, Follow-Ups, Students and Accounts are fixed-layout tables with no `minTableWidth`; at 1024 px and below the Actions (or Role) column became narrower than its button, which spilled past the scroller, cut off with its text (for example "View Details" at 1024). Fix per `cliniq-table-patterns`: `minTableWidth` from the measured need (Visits 48rem, Incidents 58rem, Follow-Ups 60rem, Students 40rem, Accounts 48rem). Inventory: its four action buttons (359px) never fit the 16.5rem column since Batches was added on Oct 17 (Batches cut and Edit invisible even at 1440); its Actions now wrap (`flex-wrap`, as Visits and Incidents already do) and the table has `minTableWidth="62.5rem"`.
- **Partly visible focus targets:** Chrome does not scroll an element that is already partly visible (a cell past a scroller's edge, a tall textarea at the fold) when it takes focus. `main.tsx` adds one document `focusin` listener that, for `:focus-visible` targets only, calls `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest', inline: 'nearest' })`, which honours every scroller's `scroll-padding`. `index.css` adds `scroll-padding-block: calc(4rem + 0.5rem) 0.5rem` on `html` so focus scrolling stops clear of the sticky 4rem top bar (sort buttons were landing under it) and of the bottom edge. `VisitCalendar` gets `scroll-px-1.5`.
- **Popovers left open behind focus:** Tab out of the date-range preset list or custom-range popover, a `DatePicker` calendar, or a `MultiSelect` panel left it open over the controls that took focus. Each now closes when focus moves to an element outside it, as an outside click already did (the custom-range popover discards its draft in that case, exactly as an outside click does).
